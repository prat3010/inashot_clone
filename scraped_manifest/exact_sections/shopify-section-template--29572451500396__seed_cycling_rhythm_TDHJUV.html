THE SEED CYCLING RHYTHM

Your body moves in a rhythm. Seed cycling is the practice of eating specific seeds at specific phases of your menstrual cycle to naturally support your hormones. It is not a cure. It is not a claim. It is thoughtful, body-aware nutrition that works with you, not against you. At In A Shot, we built a system around this practice so the right seeds find you at the right time, every month.
